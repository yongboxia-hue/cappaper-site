Support

We're here to help.

How do I add reports?

On Reports, tap the capture button or add images from Photos. Capture several pages, then confirm and save.

How do I find a report?

Browse by person on Family, or use Search to find names, hospitals, and report text.

What should I do before switching phones?

Records are stored locally. Verify your iPhone backup or export the files you need in Files before moving to another device.

Feedback and contact

Tap Feedback on the Me screen, or email yongboxia@icloud.com. Please describe the issue before sending any full medical record.
